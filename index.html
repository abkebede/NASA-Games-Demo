1. Mission Briefing

A four-person lunar south-pole crew is preparing for surface operations. Before authorizing EVA and rover activity, the mission team must evaluate the space-weather environment and determine whether the current conditions are compatible with continued operations.

5. Artemis Mission Physics Track

The Artemis mission track turns the mission timeline into a physics curriculum. Each mission stage leads to a physical principle, governing equations, a computational activity, a plot, and a student interpretation.

MISSION PHASE

Launch, Ascent, Parking Orbit, and TLI

Students study thrust, Newton's laws, gravity, orbital energy, and delta-v.

6. Building the NASA GAMES Development Team

NASA GAMES is being developed as an interdisciplinary College of Science and Technology project. The goal is to combine scientific accuracy, computational transparency, game design, education, and mission operations in one student-development platform.

Science & Engineering

Validate physics, equations, units, assumptions, mission models, subsystem couplings, and Jupyter activities.

Software & Data

Develop the browser simulation, Python/Jupyter workflows, NASA-data pipelines, shared mission state, visualization, and testing.

Game Design & Human Factors

Develop player roles, mission events, scoring, narrative, interface flow, accessibility, and decision pressure.

Education & Assessment

Develop physics lessons, student training, assessment, instructor materials, and research on student learning.

Mission Operations

Define team communication, mission procedures, go/no-go decisions, logs, pilot missions, and post-mission debriefs.

Initial Student Mission Roles

Mission Commander Flight Director Power & Thermal Engineer Life-Support Engineer Space-Weather Scientist Radiation & System-Health Engineer Communications Engineer Rover Engineer Science Lead Data & Reliability Analyst
Current development target: validate one complete chain — NASA Data → Space Weather → Radiation → System Health → Survivability — then use it as the reference architecture for the other mission modules and the first interdisciplinary team pilot.
